🖇 Message

A note becomes an entry in four moves

  1. 01

    You talk

    Voice or text, any length, no format to follow. Send it the way you'd send it to a friend at midnight.

  2. 02

    It writes

    Transcribed, cleaned into readable prose, given a title, tags, and today's date. The original wording stays available.

  3. 03

    You edit

    Change the title, retag, move the date, reformat the text. Nothing reaches Notion until you press Save.

  4. 04

    It files

    One row in your journal database. No duplicates, no orphan pages, no scattered inbox to clean up later.

It keeps notes on you

Roast
An honest read on the entry you just sent. Reply to it and the conversation keeps going — deeper, with the rest of your diary in the room.
Profile
A private knowledge base about you, built from your own notes without you filling in a single form. It posts a short line whenever it learns something new.
Rules
Tell it to stop moralising, to answer in one line, to never mention your ex. It remembers, and the rules live on a Notion page you can edit by hand.
Storage
Entries, profile, and rules all live in your Notion. We keep nothing: no copies of your notes, no accounts, no servers of ours holding your diary.
Recaps
A daily summary lands on its own. Ask for the weekly report whenever you want the longer view.

What people actually use it for

  • Break the loop you are stuck in

    Say the story you keep telling yourself out loud. It names the distortion instead of nodding along, and it has your last month of notes to prove the pattern.

  • Think a decision through

    Talk the call out before you make it. The entry keeps what you actually weighed, so the next you can check the reasoning instead of the outcome.

  • Dump the 1 a.m. spiral

    Rant in, clean entry out. The thought stops circling once it is written down, titled, and filed somewhere you can find it.

  • Review a week without homework

    No Sunday ritual to keep up. The recap is already written from what you said during the week.

Run it yourself

Open source Python. Your Telegram token, your OpenAI key, your Notion database, on a box you control. Your notes never touch our infrastructure, because there isn't any. Clone it, fill in .env, start the process — that's the whole install.
